# Cya! language

Cya! keeps a person's own thoughts and commitments, preserving their context and
letting the person decide what matters or belongs together.

## Language

**Promise**: A commitment the person intends to return to or resolve.
**Intention** is the internal name for this same concept.

**Memory**: A saved thought or original recording. Saving one does not mean the
person has committed to doing something about it.
_Avoid_: task or promise for an uncommitted thought.

**Version**: A particular expression of a memory at a point in its history. A new
version does not erase what the person previously said or believed.

**Suggested connection**: A possible relationship between saved material, offered
for the person's review. Similarity does not establish that the relationship is true.

**Accepted connection**: A relationship the person explicitly chose to keep, with
the context of the versions they were reviewing at that time.

**Branch**: A separate exploration originating from a memory or a section of one.
The source keeps its original meaning and history.

**Current priority**: What the person has chosen to return to. Cya! does not choose
it for them or prevent them from changing their mind.

**Transcript**: An editable interpretation of an original recording, rather than
a replacement for the recording or proof of exactly what was said.

**Routine**: A reusable, user-authored pattern with optional usual weekdays,
preferred window, steps and lighter fallback. Availability is a suggestion.

**Selected occurrence**: One explicit choice to include a routine on a local day,
with its exact version and original plan preserved. A later adjustment does not
rewrite that original choice. Outcomes are recorded by the person.

**Recovery**: Choosing a smaller plan, another day, another priority, a pause or
a skip after disruption. Cya! does not infer failure from the passage of time.

**Review**: An optional daily/weekly view of recorded actions and a personal note.
It is not a productivity score. Imported notes remain separate historical copies.
