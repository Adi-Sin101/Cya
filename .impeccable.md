# Design Context — Cya!

Design context for this project. The authoritative source is
`docs/Cya_Master_PRD_and_Development_Bible.md` §8.1 (design system) and §8.2
(approved mockups); this file is the short form, plus what was settled in the
grilling session of 2026-09-01.

## Target audience

Gen Z living across many apps daily — Messenger, WhatsApp, Discord, Slack, Gmail,
LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, GitHub, YouTube, Chrome (PRD §2.2). They think *"I'll
reply later," "I'll watch this tonight," "I'll read this after class"* — and those
thoughts vanish.

**Not** a general productivity audience. "Everyone" is the ambition, not the V1
target. Household-errand framing ("pay the electricity bill") belongs to Google
Tasks and loses there; postponed *content and conversations* is where nobody
competes.

## Use cases

Capture the moment someone decides "later" — from inside another app, in under two
seconds, without opening Cya! — and bring it back at the right time with one-tap
resolution. The primary object is an **intention** ("promise"), not a task. Success
is resurfacing and resolution behaviour, not list length (§1.2).

## Brand personality and tone

Calm, warm, quietly confident. A companion that remembers, not a taskmaster that
nags. The beaver mascot says *"I'll remember for you"* and appears **only** in
reward and empathy moments — never on the capture path.

Copy leads with what was kept before what is outstanding. The weekly digest opens
with "You kept 4 promises this week," never with a count of failures. Past the
snooze limit the app gets *quieter*, not louder — nagging is how a memory product
becomes a second backlog.

**The app is a trusted utility, not a daily habit.** The user should rarely need to
open it. Nothing in the interface may punish a quiet week — no streak counters, no
guilt states, no red. Kept-rate over streaks (ADR-011).

## Design system (PRD §8.1 — do not invent a new direction)

**Light** — Primary Sage `#2E705B` · Soft Sage `#74B69D` · Mint `#A7D7C5` ·
Success `#16A34A` · Warning `#F59E0B` · Error `#EF4444` · Background `#F7FAF8` ·
Surface `#FFFFFF` · Surface 2 `#F1F5F3` · Text `#1F2937` / `#64748B` ·
Gradient `#2E705B → #74B69D → #A7D7C5`.

**Dark** — same brand hues · Background `#0F172A` · Surface `#1E293B` ·
Surface 2 `#243137` · Text `#F8FAFC` / `#94A3B8`. Soft Sage is `primary` in dark
mode, because Sage on the dark background is only 2.9:1.

**Status colours split into fills and inks** (ADR-009). The §8.1 values are *fills*.
Never use them as text — amber on white is 2.2:1. Text uses the ink variants, which
pass 4.5:1 in both themes.

**Type** — Plus Jakarta Sans, bundled (Bold / SemiBold / Medium / Regular). Scale
runs display 44/36 → headline 30/26/22 → body 18/16/14 → label 12, with optical
tracking on the large end. Text scale is clamped 0.85–1.35.

**Shape and motion** — 12–16px radii, soft surfaces, generous spacing. `AppMotion`
tokens for duration and curve; `CyaHaptics` is a five-entry vocabulary (tap /
selection / confirm / celebrate / warn) so a buzz means something. Everything is
suppressed under reduced motion. Edge-to-edge system bars; the app's background is
the only background.

## Constraints

- Flutter, Impeller, Android-first. Riverpod + go_router + Drift.
- **Local-first, no server.** No network dependency exists and none may be added
  (§5.7, ADR-010). Identity is a device-local profile plus PIN/biometric.
- The capture path is native Kotlin and must stay engine-free and under 2s.
- Beauty and performance are co-equal acceptance criteria (§9.1), not a tradeoff.

## Anti-patterns for this project specifically

- No new aesthetic direction. Screens must sit beside Home, Garden and Detail
  without looking like a different app.
- The mascot never appears on the capture path.
- No streaks, no guilt, no red for an outstanding promise.
- No gradient text, and no white text on mint — that combination was 1.6:1 and made
  the completion ring invisible.
- Don't wrap everything in cards. Home was cut from 6 competing blocks to 4 for
  exactly this reason.
